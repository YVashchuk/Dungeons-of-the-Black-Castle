# BRIEF — TRACK F (narration research) for Gemini Deep Research
## Pre-generated neural narration for a static, non-commercial web gamebook: what is allowed, what is good, what it costs

**Context.** «Подземелья Чёрного замка» (JS remake of D. Braslavsky's 1991 gamebook) is a static site on GitHub Pages with 1221 paragraphs in Russian and the maintainer's translations into English, French and Ukrainian. Since 2026-09-09 it reads paragraphs aloud with the **device voices** (Web Speech API). The author wants a *good* voice. Two candidate routes: (A) pre-generate one audio file per paragraph per language with a neural TTS and ship the files next to the game; (B) keep device voices and improve their use. Route (C) — cloud TTS on demand — needs a key-holding server and is out of scope.

**Questions (cite primary sources for every claim; mark inferences):**

1. **Offline / open models for Russian.** Silero TTS (v3/v4/v5), Piper, Coqui XTTS-v2, F5-TTS, Kokoro, Bark: for each — licence of the *model weights* and of the *generated audio* (commercial vs non-commercial, attribution, redistribution of generated files), naturalness of Russian (cite published comparisons or listening tests), availability of Ukrainian and French voices, hardware needed to render ~1,500,000 characters (1221 paragraphs × 4 languages) on a laptop, and the state of maintenance in 2026.
2. **Commercial APIs.** OpenAI TTS (current models), ElevenLabs, Google Cloud TTS, Azure Neural TTS, Yandex SpeechKit: cost for ~1.5 M characters, whether the terms permit *redistributing* generated audio in a public non-commercial project, whether attribution or a disclosure is required, and Russian/Ukrainian voice quality.
3. **Rights in the text.** Narrating a copyrighted book is a derivative use. Given the open rights question of the text itself (Track D), what additional exposure does an audio edition create, and does anything change if the audio is generated on the user's device (Web Speech API) rather than distributed as files?
4. **Delivery on GitHub Pages.** Size budget: 1221 paragraphs of ~40 s each in Opus at 24–32 kbps ≈ how many MB per language; GitHub Pages / repository limits; lazy loading per paragraph; caching; whether a separate release asset or a CDN is the sensible host. Any known issues with `<audio>` autoplay policies on iOS and Android for narration started by a click.
5. **Recommendation.** A ranked shortlist (two or three options) with the reasoning, a minimal pipeline sketch (text → chunks → TTS → Opus → `dist/audio/<lang>/<N>.opus` → in-game player with fallback to device voices), and the licence/attribution text the project would have to display.

**Rules.** Research, not legal advice — say so once. No project files are needed beyond this brief; do not audit the game. Deliver `TTS_MEMO.md` (about 3–4 pages): executive summary, comparison table (model/service · licence · Russian quality · UK/FR · cost · redistribution), sizing, recommendation, sources.
