## I-01 · Confirmed interface plural issue requiring a coordinated template change

`renderInvModalHeader()` correctly selects English **item/items**, but appends `ui.mest_no_v_meshke_tolko` to the numeric size without selecting **slot/slots**. For one overflowing one-slot item, zero free slots and a six-slot bag, the current fragments produce:

> Found 1 item (taking up 1 slots), but the bag has only 0 free of 6.

The grammatical form is **“taking up 1 slot”**; for two or more it remains **“slots”**. Add the corresponding runtime plural selection, or adopt a coordinated count-neutral inventory template. This is a confirmed integration issue, not an optional preference. It is outside the literal-only JSON corrections because a new key/call site or a changed fragment contract is needed; existing leading/trailing spaces and punctuation have been preserved.

Underlying Russian questions are listed in `EN_REVIEW_REPORT.md`. The 13 untranslated hidden story-flag names are intentional under registry SA-02, so no empty or invented `from` entries have been created for them.
