**D-036 · Runtime fragment plural requires coordinated handling**

`renderInvModalHeader` always uses the plural beginning of `ui.mest_no_v_meshke_tolko`, even when the incoming inventory size is 1. With a full seven-slot bag, one ordinary pending item renders:

> Trouvé 1 objet (occupant 1 places), mais le sac n’a que 0 libres sur 7.

Both the UI key and its Russian counterpart are invariant fragments; the runtime correctly handles `objet/objets`, but not `place/places`. Correct readings are «occupant 1 place» and «occupant 2 places». This cannot be represented naturally by one static replacement while retaining the required join structure. The maintainer should choose a plural-aware runtime branch, or approve coordinated rephrasing of the whole capacity message. No source JS has been edited.
