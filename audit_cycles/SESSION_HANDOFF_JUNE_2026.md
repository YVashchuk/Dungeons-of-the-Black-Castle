## KEY ENGINE FACTS (for future sessions & external models)
1. **Combat in-fight helpers are NOT just the Copy spell.** Allies can be SUMMONED via held ITEMS: bell §612 → Медведь 11/9 (anywhere incl. castle); bear-fur amulet §84/§511 → Медведица 8/10 (only outside the Black castle). Distinct actors, own stats, fight by the Copy RULES, once per journey (`S.summonsUsed`, item retained). Engine: `COMBAT_ALLIES` map + `useAllyInCombat()`/`summonAllyAvailable()` + `#btn-summon-ally`/`#btn-summon-ally2` in startCombat/endCombat.
2. **`CASTLE_SECTIONS`** (game_logic.js) = the 26 hand-verified castle-interior combat paragraphs: `43,96,131,174,388,455,481,588,618,628,684,722,742,760,788,790,805,823,915,950,1050,1096,1099,1150,1163,1177`. Used ONLY to disable the amulet (bell works inside by canon). Chosen via Option 2 (curation) because the castle has NO thin neck and forest/castle are NOT graph-separable (75 of 76 combat paragraphs are forest-phase-reachable; only §684 is castle-exclusive). Excluded after route-reading: §617 (forest Застава), §191 (forest guide), §456 (forest spider-tree), §197 (forest she-bear den — the very Медведица the amulet summons).
3. **SIX item-grant mechanisms** (a "gated but never granted" claim is false unless ALL six are checked): `auto_items.items`, `auto_items.food[]`, choice/section `grants_items`, choice `grants_food`, `bet_payout.items` (materialized by `applyBetting()`), and `acquires` (via `applyChoiceAcquires()`).
4. **`post_combat:true`** on a choice = hidden during a pending fight, shown after victory (`renderChoices`). Post-victory continuations MUST carry it or they become combat-bypasses. After group_62 there are 116 such flags; a future combat paragraph needs the same treatment.
5. **Two distinct amulets:** «Медвежий амулет» (§84, summons Медведица) vs «Золотой амулет» (§390/§500/§625/§1164, blinds Барлад Дэрт). Matched by exact string — keep them distinct.
6. **Paragraph-jumping (dynamic targets):** several items make a later fork's destination `currentParagraph ± N` rather than a fixed number (fish +15, castle key +40, Book +24, ruby ring +401, bird-guide −50, mirror −13, orange +750, …). The remake ships these as **static, inventory-gated choices** (per-item conditional routing), NOT a runtime arithmetic engine — so a paragraph can have parallel exits to different fixed numbers gated on the item. Full verified table: `audit_cycles/dynamic_arithmetic_june_2026/ARITHMETIC_MAP.md` (Section A wired, Section B = data-only backlog). Do NOT propose a universal arithmetic hook (1221-renumbering puts some offsets out of range; each needs its own remap).

## WHEN BRIEFING A NEW EXTERNAL SESSION (Gemini / ChatGPT / Claude) — lessons to fold into the NEXT brief
These are reusable lessons for whoever writes the brief for a *future* investigation. (The June review briefs in `audit_cycles/external_review_june_2026/` are a finished record of that cycle — read them for example/format, but don't treat them as a live template; build a fresh brief and carry these points into it, alongside the game rules and the KEY ENGINE FACTS above.)
- **Gemini MUST be given its own pre-extracted data folder, NOT the raw repo.** `remake_data.js` (single ~1 MB line) and `mj_art.js` (~6.9 MB base64) overflow Gemini's context window and its reader silently returns only a fragment — this happened in the June cycle and produced confabulated castle guesses. The fix is a shard pack: the paragraph digest split into ~300-paragraph JSONL files, a precomputed graph-facts JSON (reachability/dangling/combat list/mismatches), a combat-paragraphs-with-inbound file, and a text-only art-coverage extract. ChatGPT can hit the same limit on the 1 MB blob — give it the same pack if its reader truncates. (Build the pack fresh each time from current sources; the June one was deliberately not kept in-repo.)
- **Spell out the easy-to-mis-flag mechanics in the new brief** (all in KEY ENGINE FACTS above): (a) a summoned ally is NOT a Copy — it has its OWN Skill/Stamina and only borrows the Copy *resolution rules*; (b) the bear-fur amulet is castle-scoped — powerless inside the castle is canon, not a bug (curated `CASTLE_SECTIONS`); (c) paragraph-jumping items route via static inventory-gated choices — don't "fix" them or propose a universal arithmetic hook; (d) items are granted by SIX mechanisms (incl. `acquires`).
