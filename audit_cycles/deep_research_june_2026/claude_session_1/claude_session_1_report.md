# Source-Critical Restoration of «Подземелья Чёрного замка» (1991): Findings Report

## TL;DR
- The 1991 first edition (М.: АТ, ISBN 5-87986-001-9, 223 pp., tirage 100 000, 617 paragraphs) survives publicly ONLY as an image-PDF scan on quest-book.ru; no OCR/typeset of the 1991 text was located, so verbatim 1991 quotes for the three cases could NOT be produced this session.
- The freely-readable online full-texts (lib.ru, coollib, royallib, knigosite) are all the 1995 SECOND edition (ЛОВКОСТЬ/СИЛА/ОБАЯНИЕ stats, Элгариол, 651 paragraphs) — a different recension from both the 1991 original and the 1221-paragraph remaster.
- Case 3's "friend" is firmly identified as the caged mage ХЭРНОК; the hidden-arithmetic mechanics (вычтите N / letter-sum) are almost certainly a SECOND-edition (1995) invention, since the 1991 first edition used direct paragraph references, not arithmetic codes.

## Key Findings (edition landscape — the decisive context)
The single most important discovery is that "the text of «Подземелья Чёрного замка»" is not one text but a chain of substantially different recensions, and the verbatim quotes the project needs must be matched to the correct recension:

- **1991 first edition** — М.: АТ, ISBN 5-87986-001-9, 223 pp., 70×108/32, soft cover, tirage 100 000, illustrations A. Sukhorukov. Stats: МАСТЕРСТВО/ВЫНОСЛИВОСТЬ/УДАЧА. **617 paragraphs**, a figure tesera.ru (article/95286) attributes to Braslavsky himself: «По словам самого Д. Браславского, написание книги заняло у него не больше месяца… объём книги — 617 параграфов (в первом издании)». Item use via DIRECT paragraph references (not arithmetic). Kingdom unnamed. Available publicly only as a 5 MB image-PDF scan (`black_tower_91.pdf`) on quest-book.ru.
- **1995 second edition** — series «Путь героя», ISBN 5-214-00107-X, tirage 35 000 (per the gamebooks.ru publisher blog: «Тираж книги более скромный — 35 000 экземпляров»; Russian Wikipedia's «Путь героя» article instead gives series tirages reaching 40 000). **651 paragraphs**, verbatim per tesera.ru: «Увеличилось общее количество параграфов — с шестисот семнадцати до шестисот пятидесяти одного». Stats renamed ЛОВКОСТЬ/СИЛА; new parameters ОБАЯНИЕ and ЛОЯЛЬНОСТЬ added; UDACHA check changed. Setting moved into Элгариол; password named «Хобгоблин». Item use converted to a "secret code" (+/− a number to the paragraph). THIS is the text on lib.ru and the FB2 mirrors. NOTE: this edition carries its own well-documented fatal misprint, per ru.wikipedia «Путь героя»: «Во втором издании существует опечатка, которая может сделать непроходимой всю игру. Начиная с 590 параграфа все параграфы имеют номер на единицу меньше, чем нужно».
- **2010 third edition** — Чебоксары: Селиванов, ISBN 978-5-905195-01-3.
- **2014 fourth edition** — Чебоксары: Селиванов, ISBN 978-5-905195-11-2, hardcover, 285 pp., tirage 500. Heavily rewritten: goblins/orcs removed and replaced by «гаррасы»; action fully relocated to Элгариол.
- **2023 fifth edition** — gamebooks.ru, ISBN 978-5-905195-30-3. Publisher states it "returned to the text of the 1991 first edition," restoring Green Knights, goblins and orcs that the 4th edition had removed.
- **Modern remaster (FB2, 1221 paragraphs)** — the project's working canon; not investigated here per the brief.

This matters directly: the remaster's §887/§734 face-6 anomaly (Case 1) and its «вычтите 35»/letter-sum mechanics (Case 3) must be checked against the 1991 original, but the only freely machine-readable text is the 1995 recension, which already differs in numbering and item mechanics.

## CASE 1 — The Goblin dice game: the face-6 anomaly
### FOUND
- F1.1: The goblin dice-gambling scene exists and is set in a guard hut (сторожка) in the castle courtyard. [SOURCE: http://www.lib.ru/RUFANT/BRASWASLKIJ/blacktower_game.txt] «...Справа, немного в стороне от ворот, видна сторожка, откуда слышны веселые голоса Гоблинов и звон стаканов.» — Edition: 1995 second edition (identified by ЛОВКОСТЬ/СИЛА/ОБАЯНИЕ stat system and Элгариол setting) — Confidence: Medium (verbatim fragment recovered via the search index of the lib.ru full text; full dice-router paragraph not retrieved).
- F1.2: A community walkthrough confirms goblins AND orcs gamble at dice in a courtyard hut, and notes the gate-orcs (a different group) "know nothing about the dice game." [SOURCE: https://quest-book.ru/forum/topic/386] «Стражники-орки на воротах замка ничего не знают о существовании игры " в кости". … во дворе сторожка, где 2 гоблина и 2 орка вполне спокойно в эту игру режутся» — Edition: discussion of the 1991/1995 text — Confidence: High.

### INFERRED
- The 1991 first edition almost certainly used DIRECT paragraph references for the dice outcomes (not the remaster's hidden router structure), because per tesera.ru (article/95286): «Большинство предметов получило "секретный код"… тогда как в первом издании на их использование применялась прямая ссылка». The remaster's four "router" paragraphs are therefore a remaster-era reconstruction of a mechanic that in 1991 was a set of plain "Шесть? — N" links. Whether the face-6 → key/door (§734-analogue) mis-link already existed in 1991 cannot be determined without the scan.
- Because the 1995 edition renumbered everything (651 §§) and rebuilt the item economy, the face-6 anomaly should be checked in the 1991 scan FIRST; a match in the 1995 text would be only weak corroboration. Note also the 1995 edition's own off-by-one misprint (paragraphs ≥590 numbered one too low) — a reminder that this title has a documented history of numbering errors, lending plausibility to the remaster's face-6 anomaly being an inherited printing slip.

### NOT FOUND
- The verbatim item-stake dice list (Q1.1) in any edition.
- The verbatim face-6 target paragraph in the 1991 or 1995 text (Q1.2).
- Any published errata list or forum thread discussing this specific face-6 → key/door mis-link (Q1.3). The much-discussed "fatal errors" in the book are unrelated to the dice router: (a) the 1st-edition deer-hide/§144 unreachability and (b) the 2nd-edition ≥590 off-by-one.
- A walkthrough fragment quoting the six dice outcomes verbatim (Q1.4).

### LEADS
- `black_tower_91.pdf` (1991) and `black_tower_95.pdf` (1995) on quest-book.ru (download IDs 2095/2094) — the dice scene and its outcomes are in there; needs OCR of the 1991 scan.
- The "Игровая Матрица" CD text engine and the Subreal-Team / telegram-bot reimplementations encode the full branching and could be queried for the dice router targets.

## CASE 2 — Two amulets, the little mirror, and the pass
### FOUND
- F2.1: In the post-battle "fugitives" variant of the 1995 edition, the dropped items are a golden whistle, a little mirror, and a Black-castle emblem — the mirror carries the secret code (−83), NOT a "golden amulet (−333)". [SOURCE: http://www.lib.ru/RUFANT/BRASWASLKIJ/blacktower_game.txt] «Вы так и не узнаете, кто были эти люди, но зато можете подобрать золотой свисток (+ 156), зеркальце (- 83) и небольшую эмблему в виде Черного замка (+ 59), оброненные ими во время боя. Скорее всего, это Беглецы из замка, и тогда вы на правильном пути - 3.» — Edition: 1995 second edition — Confidence: High (verbatim).
- F2.2: The she-bear summon ally exists and is discussed by the author as a moral test; the bear "is stingy" and lets the hero take only one reward item. [SOURCE: https://tesera.ru/user/Jumangee/thought/417299/] «Оригинальным ходом можно назвать возможность вызова на помощь союзника — медведицы. Сам Браславский утверждает, что данная возможность — это одновременно и проверка моральных качеств игрока… а медведица, по-видимому, просто жадничает.» — Edition: critical article covering 1st/2nd editions — Confidence: High.

### INFERRED
- The 1995 second edition does NOT use the remaster's «вычтите 333» golden-amulet detector at this scene; instead the little mirror carries the code (−83). This strongly suggests the remaster's "golden amulet (вычтите 333)" is a remaster-era elaboration (or a 1991-specific element that the 1995 edition reworked), and that the project should treat the two-amulet system (bear-fur = summon; golden = detector/blinder) as something to verify specifically against the 1991 scan rather than assume continuity.
- Given the 1995 edition's "one item only" bear reward, the bear-fur amulet is the reward branch (alternative to пояс/шкура), consistent with the remaster; this is structurally continuous and low-risk.

### NOT FOUND
- The verbatim debt-payment paragraph and whether it reads «золотым амулетом» or «амулетом» (Q2.1) in any edition.
- Any scene treating the BEAR-FUR amulet as tradable/payable (Q2.2).
- The verbatim bear-cub healing grant scene and the verbatim golden-amulet grant scene from the original (Q2.3).
- The verbatim зеркальце-as-пропуск knight scene and whether it uses the word «пропуск» (Q2.4).
- A complete enumeration of зеркальце uses from the original (Q2.5).
- The paper Пропуск / Начальник стражи / «вычтите 40» paragraph (Q2.4) in verbatim form.

### LEADS
- The recovered (−83) code on the mirror and (+156)/(+59) on whistle/emblem give exact 1995 secret-code values that can be cross-checked against the 1991 scan to see whether 1991 used direct links instead.
- lib.ru full text (decoded as Windows-1251) will contain all зеркальце occurrences; the encoding, not availability, was the blocker.

## CASE 3 — The fugitives' friend (the last unwired mechanics)
### FOUND
- F3.1: The fugitives (Беглецы) appear at paragraph 520 of the 1995 edition; they give the whistle and mirror and teach the password «Трое из Эвенло» (Эвенло = their village). [SOURCE: https://quest-book.ru/forum/topic/386] «Заветные слова «Трое из Эвенло» говорят беглецы из замка. (параграф 520)» — Edition: fan analysis of the 1995 second edition — Confidence: High (paragraph location); the full paragraph text was not recovered verbatim.
- F3.2: The fugitives' friend — the man "turned into an old man" held in the castle — is the mage ХЭРНОК, encountered in a cage (клетка) in the dungeon; the hero reaches him via the "two old men / stone-bag" cell route by whispering «Трое из Эвенло» to the old jailer. [SOURCE: https://quest-book.ru/forum/topic/386] «вы пройдете мимо клетки, где сидит Хэрнок, а потом мимо клетки с оленем.» — Edition: fan analysis of the 1995 second edition — Confidence: High.
- F3.3: Хэрнок is corroborated as a recurring Braslavsky character — the mage Хэрнок reappears "in the flesh" in the France-set later book of the series. [SOURCE: https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Путь_героя] «один из второстепенных персонажей «Подземелья Чёрного замка», маг Хэрнок, встречается во плоти и помогает главному герою приблизиться к разгадке тайны своей семьи» (the same article notes that book also contains a Барлад Дэрт easter-egg). — Edition: series overview — Confidence: High.
- F3.4: The 1991 first edition used DIRECT paragraph references for item/secret use; the +/− arithmetic ("secret code") system was introduced only in the 1995 second edition. [SOURCE: https://tesera.ru/article/95286/] «"безымянный" в первом издании пароль в замок стал конкретным словом… Большинство предметов получило «секретный код»… тогда как в первом издании на их использование применялась прямая ссылка.» — Edition: critical article (explicitly comparing 1st vs 2nd) — Confidence: High.

### INFERRED
- Because the 1991 first edition used direct references and the arithmetic "secret code" was a 1995 invention, the remaster's hidden-arithmetic friend mechanics — «вычтите 35» at the meeting site (§390) and the name-letter-sum «+30» (§435) — almost certainly did NOT exist in the 1991 original in that form. They are most plausibly inherited from the 1995 secret-code lineage (or newly engineered for the remaster), not restored from 1991. (Answers Q3.6: the MECHANICS as hidden arithmetic are a post-1991 feature; the underlying SCENE — deliver «Трое из Эвенло» to Хэрнок for a reward — does originate in 1991.)
- Letter-sum cross-check (informational, not verified against book): with the stated convention (А=1…Я=33), ХЭРНОК = Х(23)+Э(31)+Р(18)+Н(15)+О(16)+К(12) = 115; +30 = 145. This is suggestive but cannot be validated without the remaster's actual destination paragraph, since numbering differs across editions — treat as a lead only.
- Хэрнок's payoff is most likely the secret needed for the endgame (the mirror/passage secret or the golden-ring/amulet route), consistent with community descriptions that the prisoner-route yields the key endgame information.

### NOT FOUND
- Verbatim text of the fugitives' scene from the 1991 original (Q3.1).
- Хэрнок's verbatim cell paragraph(s), his exact paragraph number, and the verbatim reward/message-delivery text (Q3.2, Q3.4, Q3.5).
- Any paragraph applying «вычтите 35» or a name-letter-sum in the 1991 or 1995 text, verbatim (Q3.3).
- Confirmation of exactly what delivering the message changes (reward/info/ending) in verbatim form (Q3.5).

### LEADS
- The Хэрнок cell paragraph and the fugitives' §520 are both in the lib.ru 1995 text (decode as Windows-1251) and in the 1991 scan; OCR/decoding will yield the verbatim message-delivery wording.
- The later series book in which Хэрнок reappears may restate the «Трое из Эвенло» backstory and could corroborate the message content.

## Recommendations
1. **Treat the 1991 scan as the only authoritative primary source and OCR it.** Download `black_tower_91.pdf` (quest-book.ru download/2095) and run Russian OCR; this is the single action that unblocks every verbatim quote for all three cases. Benchmark: a clean OCR of the dice-hut paragraphs and the Хэрнок cell decides Cases 1 and 3 outright.
2. **Decode the lib.ru text as Windows-1251** (the bytes are intact; only the UTF-8 assumption corrupted them) to obtain the 1995 second-edition verbatim text immediately, as a corroborating recension. Use it to extract: the item-stake dice router, the bear grant scenes, the debt-payment wording, all зеркальце occurrences, the Пропуск/Начальник стражи paragraph, the §520 fugitives, and the Хэрнок cell. When citing 1995 numbers, remember the ≥590 off-by-one slip.
3. **For Case 1**, after OCR, compare the 1991 face-6 target against the 1995 and remaster targets. If 1991 also mis-points face-6 to a key/door scene, the misprint is original; if not, it was introduced in 1995 or the remaster. Threshold to change conclusion: a verbatim 1991 six-face list.
4. **For Case 3**, record that the hidden-arithmetic mechanics (вычтите 35 / letter-sum +30) are a post-1991 feature; restore the SCENE (deliver «Трое из Эвенло» to Хэрнок) using the 1991 text, and wire the arithmetic from the 1995/remaster lineage only after confirming Хэрнок's destination paragraph. The letter-sum ХЭРНОК(А=1…Я=33)+30 = 145 is a hypothesis to test against the remaster graph, not a fact.
5. **For the editions inventory**, obtain paragraph counts for the 2010, 2014 and 2023 print editions from their colophons (the 2023 fifth edition, marketed as a return to the 1991 text, is the best modern proxy for 1991 numbering and should be acquired).

## Caveats
- No verbatim 1991 text was obtained; the primary deliverable (verbatim 1991 quotes) is NOT FOUND this session, blocked by (a) the 1991 text existing only as an un-OCR'd image-PDF and (b) the readable 1995 text being served in Windows-1251 and in over-limit pages.
- All freely-readable "full texts" online are the 1995 SECOND edition, which differs from the 1991 original in numbering (651 vs 617), stat names, and item mechanics — do NOT treat lib.ru/coollib/royallib as the 1991 text.
- The Хэрнок identification, the §520 fugitives location, and the dice-hut setting come from a high-quality fan walkthrough and a series wiki, not from a verbatim book scan; they are reliable for scene-matching but should be confirmed against the OCR'd primary text before being quoted as canon.
- Paragraph numbers cited (520, 464, 144) are 1995-edition numbers and must NOT be matched across editions by number — only by scene text, per project methodology. The 1995 edition additionally carries its own ≥590 off-by-one misprint, so even within that edition printed numbers above 589 are unreliable.
- The letter-sum arithmetic for ХЭРНОК is presented as an untested hypothesis; it has not been validated against the book.
