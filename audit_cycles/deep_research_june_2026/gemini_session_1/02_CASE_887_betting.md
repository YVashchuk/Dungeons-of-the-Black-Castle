# CASE 1 — The Goblin dice game: the face-6 anomaly

## Background (remaster state)
In the Goblin gambling scene the hero can bet gold or items and roll a die. The remaster has four «router» paragraphs mapping the rolled face (1–6) to outcome paragraphs. Three routers map all six faces to plausible win/lose outcomes. The fourth — the **item-stake router, §887 in the remaster** — maps faces 1–5 to betting outcomes but **face 6 to §734**, a key-and-door scene from a completely different location (its only legitimate entry is the corridor scene §694). It is the lone non-betting target among all 24 router faces — almost certainly a misprint somewhere in the chain 1991 → remaster.

A verbatim 1991 quote of the six-outcome list (Q1.1) decides the case by itself. Errata or walkthrough fragments are second-best. «The original has the same anomaly» is also a valid, decisive finding — but only with a verbatim quote.
