# DEEP RESEARCH BRIEF — Session 1
## «Подземелья Чёрного замка» (Дмитрий Браславский, 1991): source recovery + three canon questions

Project: a source-critical PWA remake of the first Russian gamebook (see 01_PROJECT_CONTEXT.md). June 2026.

## MISSION

**PRIMARY — locate the text of the FIRST edition:** Дмитрий Браславский, «Подземелья Чёрного замка», Москва, 1991. Reference page: https://fantlab.ru/edition25230 . Any form counts: book scans (PDF/DjVu — e.g. rutracker, archive.org), OCR text, fan re-typesets, partially photographed pages, library digitizations, and text fragments quoted in forums or walkthroughs.

**SECONDARY — answer the three case question-sets** (files 02–04): by direct verbatim quotation from the 1991 text where possible; otherwise via community errata, forum discussions, and walkthroughs («прохождение»). Walkthroughs are **first-class evidence**: they quote paragraph numbers, scene details and exact mechanics.

**TERTIARY — editions inventory:** every known edition/reprint of the book (the 1991 first edition; later reprints; the modern remastered re-edition), with paragraph counts where discoverable.

## WHAT WE ALREADY HAVE — do NOT report as findings
We possess the modern remastered re-edition in FB2 (**1221 paragraphs**, the corrected text; flibusta b/532645). Every quote in files 02–04 comes from it. The research target is the **1991 original** (or hard evidence about it), to resolve discrepancies the remaster inherited or introduced.

## CRITICAL METHODOLOGY
1. **Paragraph numbers differ between editions.** The remaster has 1221 paragraphs; the 1991 original's numbering is different/unknown. NEVER match across editions by number. Match scenes by TEXT — the case files give verbatim remake quotes as match keys.
2. **Verbatim or nothing.** Quote sources verbatim. Never reconstruct a quote from memory, never paraphrase inside «…». If a scan page is partly unreadable, say so explicitly.
3. **Edition identification per quote.** Every quote must state which edition/printing it comes from and how that was determined.
4. **Search in RUSSIAN.** Seed queries: «Браславский Подземелья Чёрного замка 1991 текст», «Подземелья Чёрного замка книга-игра скачать», «Подземелья Чёрного замка прохождение», «Браславский книга-игра опечатка ошибка параграф», plus case-specific phrases from files 02–04.
5. **Candidate venues:** fantlab.ru (edition page, author page, forums), quest-book.ru (Russian gamebook community + archives), archive.org, rutracker.org (scans), VK communities «книги-игры», samlib.ru / lib.ru, author interviews, old forum/FIDO archives.

## THE THREE CASES (read files 02–04 in full)
1. **02_CASE_887_betting.md** — the Goblin dice game: one of six die-face outcomes in the remaster jumps into an unrelated key-door scene. What did the 1991 original have?
2. **03_CASE_amulets_mirror_pass.md** — two amulets, the little mirror that «оказывается пропуском», and the purchased paper Пропуск: how does the original treat them?
3. **04_CASE_fugitives_friend.md** — the fugitives ask the hero to deliver a message to their friend («вычтете 35»; name letter-sum «прибавьте 30»): who is the friend in the original, what is the NAME, where is the meeting?
